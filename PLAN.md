# Plan: Savoir French redesign

## What I am building

A better website for Savoir French, a French bakery and cafe on 
ANU campus at 105 Ellery Crescent. Their current site is 
https://savoirfrench.com.au and it is bad at its main job which 
is telling people where the cafe is and when it is open.

## Why I picked this cafe

I had a meeting here once and could not find it because my phone 
was dead. I opened the website on my laptop and it was useless. 
The first thing I saw was a pick up order button. There was no 
directions, no entrance info, nothing. The entrance is through 
a building lobby and the site never says that.

My redesign fixes this. If you open it on a dead phone using 
someone else's laptop you should be able to find the cafe in 
under 10 seconds.

## Colours

Cream background: #F5F0E8
Dark text: #2C2C2C
Gold for buttons and highlights: #C9A84C

## Pages I am building

Homepage: who they are, where they are, opening hours, 
a button to order that goes to their real site, and a 
map with a note saying "enter via the ANU School of Art 
and Design building lobby"

About us: their story and what they make

Catering: what they offer, email link to get in touch

Gift cards: explains gift cards, links to real site to buy

## What links back to their real site

Ordering, buying gift cards, anything that involves money 
or accounts. I am not building any of that.

## Photos

Using free Unsplash photos for now as placeholders. 
French bakery, croissants, cafe interior.

## Footer note on every page

"This is a student redesign made for COMP4020 at ANU. 
It is not the official Savoir French website."

## What I am not building

No shopping cart, no login, no booking system, no 
newsletter. None of that. Just information pages.
